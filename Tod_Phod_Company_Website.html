Who we are

A little idea for a cleaner home

Tod Phod is a student science-project concept: a smart helper that brings everyday floor-cleaning tasks together in one friendly robot.

Our idea combines movement, sensors and cleaning tools to show how technology can help solve everyday problems.

Our mission

To imagine useful and responsible technology that can make daily home tasks easier.

Clean Home, Happy Home!
What it can do

Meet the cleaning features

These are the planned features of our project model.

🧹

Sweeping

Rotating brushes help gather dust and small crumbs.

🌪️

Dust collection

A proposed vacuum unit collects loose dirt into a dust box.

🧽

Mopping

A reusable mop pad is designed to wipe suitable hard floors.

📡

Smart sensors

Sensors can help notice dirt and detect nearby obstacles.

🛞

Smart movement

Wheels help it travel around open areas and furniture.

🔋

Rechargeable power

A rechargeable battery is part of the design idea.

The process

How Tod Phod works

A simple four-step explanation of the planned cleaning cycle.

1

Sense

Scans the nearby floor and looks for obstacles.

2

Move

Wheels guide the robot through the cleaning area.

3

Clean

Brushes, dust collection and a mop pad do their jobs.

4

Finish

Stops when the cleaning task is complete.
